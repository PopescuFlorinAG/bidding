# CONTEXT — bidding vocabulary

<!-- graffino-agentic:begin v1 -->
> The approved terms for this project. Use each term exactly as defined, in the part
> of speech given. Never swap in a synonym; never use a word on an _Avoid_ line.
<!-- graffino-agentic:end -->

- **Auction** (noun) — A public sale in which goods or services are sold to the highest bidder.
  _Avoid_: tender, sale, listing

- **Bid** (noun) — An offer of a specific monetary amount made by a participant to purchase a lot.
  _Avoid_: proposal, bet, stake

- **Bidder** (noun) — A registered user who places bids on active auctions.
  _Avoid_: buyer, client, competitor

- **Seller** (noun) — A registered user who creates auctions and offers items for bidding.
  _Avoid_: vendor, merchant, owner

- **Lot** (noun) — An individual item or group of items put up for sale in a single auction.
  _Avoid_: article, product, entity

- **Reserve Price** (noun) — The confidential minimum price that must be met for a lot to be sold.
  _Avoid_: floor price, base threshold, bottom price

- **Winning Bid** (noun) — The highest accepted bid at the close of an auction meeting all reserve conditions.
  _Avoid_: top bid, final price, closing offer
